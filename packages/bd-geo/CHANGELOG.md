# bd-geo data changelog

Unit IDs are never removed or reused. See [README.md](README.md#changing-the-data).

## 2026.1 (2026-10-21)

- First version: 8 divisions, 64 districts, 495 upazilas and 12 city corporations from BBS geocodes (HDX cod-ab-bgd v03, valid from 2023-05-20), Bangla names from nuhil/bangladesh-geocode.
- Added Dhaka's 50 metropolitan thanas (`BD3026M01`–`M50`) and Chattogram's 15 (`BD2015M01`–`M15`).
- Renamed upazilas use their current names, with the old ones as aliases: Indurkani (Zianagar), Shantiganj (South Sunamganj), Rajibpur (Char Rajibpur), Adarsha Sadar (Comilla Sadar).
- Common area names as aliases: Ashulia (Savar), Tongi (Gazipur Sadar), Fatullah and Siddhirganj (Narayanganj Sadar), Bashundhara (Vatara), Agargaon (Sher-e-Bangla Nagar), Farmgate (Tejgaon), Azimpur (Lalbagh), Agrabad (Double Mooring).
